SMS Consent & Opt-In Process

How customers opt in to text messages

Montana Timberline Firewood sends transactional SMS messages only to customers who provide a mobile phone number and give affirmative consent to receive text messages about their firewood order or service.

  1. The customer completes the in-person customer information and SMS consent form at Montana Timberline Firewood.
  2. The customer provides their name and mobile phone number.
  3. The SMS consent checkbox starts unchecked. The customer must actively check the box to opt in.
  4. Staff enter the customer name and mobile number into the Montana Timberline customer database and record SMS consent separately.
  5. Transactional SMS messages are sent only when a valid mobile number is on file and the customer's SMS Consent status is recorded as Yes.
  6. If the consent box is not checked, consent is declined, or no consent is recorded, the customer is not sent SMS messages.
Consent is optional and is not a condition of purchasing firewood or receiving service. Message frequency varies based on customer orders. Message and data rates may apply. Customers may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance.

Types of messages customers may receive

Messages are limited to transactional and customer-service communications related to the customer's firewood order or service. Examples include notice that a trailer or firewood order has been loaded, order status, payment information, pickup availability, and responses to customer service questions.

Montana Timberline Firewood does not use this consent to send unrelated promotional or marketing messages.
