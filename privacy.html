Montana TimberLine FireWood Co. ("Montana TimberLine FireWood Co.," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy.   • Your name or business name

We may use your information to:

  • Meet legal, accounting, regulatory, and recordkeeping requirements

SMS/Text Messaging

If you provide your mobile number and consent to receive text messages from Montana TimberLine FireWood Co., we may send you transactional and customer-service text messages related to your dealings with us. These may include order updates, trailer-loading notifications, pickup information, payment-related information, and responses to customer inquiries.

Message frequency varies depending on your transactions and communications with Montana TimberLine FireWood Co. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. You may reply HELP for assistance.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing goods or services from Montana TimberLine FireWood Co.

Mobile Information and SMS Consent

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number, SMS opt-in information, or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

We do not sell your personal information.

Data Security

Data Retention

We may retain customer, transaction, and communication information for as long as reasonably necessary for business, accounting, legal, regulatory, and recordkeeping purposes.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request that we update or correct personal information we maintain about you.

For SMS communications, you may withdraw your consent at any time by replying STOP to a Montana TimberLine FireWood Co. text message.
