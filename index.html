The process

Four stops, start to finish

Everything is weighed, not eyeballed — so what you pay always matches what's actually in your trailer.

01

Pull onto the scale

Drive your empty trailer up. We record its starting weight before anything gets loaded.

02

Tell us what you need

Pick your wood or sawdust and how much you're after. We'll point you to the right pile.

03

We fill your trailer

We load it on the spot with exactly what you ordered — no pre-bagged bundles to guess at.

04

Weigh out & pay

Back on the scale. The difference is your net weight — that's what determines your total.
