The process

Four stops, start to finish

Everything is weighed, not eyeballed — so what you pay always matches what's actually in your trailer.

01

Pull onto the scale

Drive your empty trailer up. We record its starting weight before anything gets loaded.

02

Tell us what you need

Pick your wood or sawdust and how much you're after. We'll point you to the right pile.

03

We fill your trailer

We load it on the spot with exactly what you ordered — no pre-bagged bundles to guess at.

04

Weigh out & pay

Back on the scale. The difference is your net weight — that's what determines your total.

Cordwood — Softwood Mix

White Wood

A mixed softwood pile — Grand Fir, White Pine, Yellow Pine, Bull Pine, Spruce, Lodgepole Pine, and Cedar. Lighter by weight and easier on the wallet than our Red Wood mix, White Wood catches quick and burns clean — a great everyday choice for stoves, fireplaces, and weekend bonfires.

A mixed pile of Douglas-fir and Western Larch (Tamarack) — denser wood that burns hotter and longer than White Wood. Built for cold Montana nights and anyone who wants a fire that holds through the evening.

Our story

Twenty years of wood, one hard season, a fresh start

This land in the Flathead Valley started out as a dairy farm. It was later converted into MontanaTimberline, a wood processing and bundling plant that supplied pallets of firewood to homes across the Pacific Northwest for close to twenty years — heat for the winter, wood for the weekend bonfire, and everything in between.

On December 2025, a fire moved through our warehouse. Over three days, the building and everything in it were lost. It was a hard season for our crew and for the community we'd built alongside.

What came out of it is a simpler, more direct way of doing business. Instead of pre-bundled pallets, we now sell loose cordwood and sawdust straight off the lot — local, good-quality wood, weighed fair and sold honest, one trailer at a time.
